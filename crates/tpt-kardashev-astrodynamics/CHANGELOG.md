# Changelog: tpt-kardashev-astrodynamics

## Unreleased

- `adaptive::AdaptiveCowell`: adaptive propagation with the Dormand–Prince 5(4) embedded Runge–Kutta pair. Steps are accepted when the scaled RMS error over position and velocity is at most one; the next step follows the fifth-root rule, growing by at most five and shrinking by at least ten. Tests in `tests/adaptive.rs`: three orbits of an eccentric orbit against the analytic solution (5e-7 km with 1e-12 tolerances, bound 1e-6 km), tighter tolerances giving smaller errors, energy drift 4e-12 over five orbits, backward propagation, zero duration, and rejection of invalid tolerances. New error `InvalidTolerance`.
- `symplectic::Verlet` and `symplectic::verlet_step`: velocity Verlet, a second-order, time-reversible integrator for accelerations that depend only on position. Its energy error stays bounded over long arcs: over a thousand orbits the per-orbit peak was unchanged between the first and last hundred orbits (5.8e-3 at 50 steps per orbit, 3.6e-4 at 200), where RK4 at 50 steps per orbit grew tenfold. Tests in `tests/symplectic.rs`: error ratio 4.00 on halving the step against the analytic two-body solution, energy bounds, angular momentum 1.5e-14 over a hundred orbits, round trip, zero duration, and rejection of invalid steps. The method does not check that the force is position-only.
- `force::ZonalHarmonics`: zonal harmonics of degrees 2 to 4, with the coefficients supplied by the caller. With J3 and J4 zero it reduces to `J2Zonal`. Tests in `tests/zonal.rs`: agreement with `J2Zonal` to 1e-12; the closed form matches the finite-difference gradient of the geopotential for J2, J3 and J4 (1e-6); the J3 equatorial closed form; the parity of J3 under reflection through the equator. J3 and J4 WGS 84 constants are not bundled.
- Tests in `tests/frames.rs` for the ecliptic–equatorial round trip, RTN components mapped back to the inertial state over 192 element sets, and agreement of the RTN normal with the perifocal W axis. Test in `tests/lambert.rs`: a quarter turn of an eccentric ellipse, with the time of flight from Kepler's equation, returns the element-set velocities.
- `tests/parser_robustness.rs`: deterministic robustness checks for `sgp4::parse_tle` and `spk::read_segments`, with a fixed-seed generator: mutated, random and truncated element sets, and random bytes with the DAF magic. None panics. This is not a coverage-guided fuzzer.
- `tests/angular_momentum.rs`: the angular-momentum vector over twenty orbits of an eccentric orbit under `AdaptiveCowell` at 1e-12 tolerances, relative drift 1.7e-11 (bound 1e-10).

## Unreleased

- `Error` (`#[non_exhaustive]`): non-finite input, invalid μ, non-elliptic orbit, degenerate state.
- `Kilometres`, `Seconds`, `Radians`: finite-checked unit newtypes; `Vec3` with dot, cross, add, subtract and scale.
- `Keplerian` elements and `StateVector`: `from_state` and `to_state` for elliptic two-body orbits, with the singular-case conventions in the README. Tests in `tests/elements.rs`: known values, the angular-momentum and energy identities, round trips over a grid of 2160 element sets, and rejection of invalid input.
- The element conversions are behind the default `std` feature. The `no_std` build compiles the error, unit and vector types only.
- `time`: `Epoch` on the TT scale (seconds past J2000), with TAI, Julian dates, and conversion to and from `UtcTime` through a caller-supplied `LeapSeconds` table (`LeapEntry` rows). Calendar arithmetic is integer-only, so the module is `no_std`. Tests in `tests/time.rs`. TDB is not written.
- `Error`: `InvalidCalendar`, `NoLeapSecondEntry`, `InvalidLeapSeconds`.
- `Mat3` (`no_std`): 3×3 matrix with multiply, transpose, determinant and vector product.
- `Keplerian::perifocal_to_inertial`: the rotation Rz(Ω)·Rx(i)·Rz(ω). `to_state` uses it.
- `frames::rtn_matrix`: the radial, transverse and normal axes of a state. Tests in `tests/frames.rs`: orthonormality and handedness, closed-form perifocal components, RTN components of position and velocity, circular velocity along T, and rejection of degenerate and non-finite states. LVLH, the heliocentric ecliptic and the Earth-fixed frame are not written.
- `propagate::propagate`: two-body propagation by universal variables. Stumpff functions (series for |z| < 1, closed forms otherwise), Newton's method on the universal Kepler equation, and the f and g coefficients. Tests in `tests/propagate.rs`: a full circular period returns to the start, half a period gives the antipode, energy and angular momentum are conserved, propagation composes and reverses, the result agrees with an independent solve of Kepler's equation, and hyperbolic states propagate. New error `NoConvergence`.
- `force::ForceModel` trait, with `force::CentralBody` (point-mass gravity) and tuple sums `(A, B)`. `integrate::rk4_step` (classical RK4 on position and velocity) and `integrate::Cowell` (fixed step, last step shortened to land on the end time, either direction). Tests in `tests/cowell.rs`: RK4 error falls as the fourth power of the step (1.4e-10 at 5 s; 16.4× per halving), a full circular period closes, energy drift over one period is small, sums of models add, backward integration, and rejection of invalid input. New error `InvalidStep`.
- `force::J2Zonal`: the J2 zonal perturbation, with the WGS 84 constants `WGS84_J2` and `WGS84_EQUATORIAL_RADIUS_KM`. Tests in `tests/j2.rs`: closed-form equatorial and polar accelerations; zero J2 gives none; the nodal regression of an orbit integrated for ten periods matches the secular rate −(3/2)·n·J2·(R/p)²·cos i to 0.2%; without J2 the node does not drift; and rejection of invalid input.
- `manoeuvre`: `hohmann`, `bi_elliptic` (through an intermediate radius that must exceed both ends) and `plane_change` (law of cosines, which gives 2·v·sin(θ/2) for a pure plane change). Returns Δv magnitudes and transfer times. Tests in `tests/manoeuvre.rs`: each transfer is propagated to its next apsis and the radius and speed checked; the LEO-to-GEO Hohmann total (3.8926 km/s; dv1 2.4257, dv2 1.4669) and transfer time (about 18 990 s); symmetry; bi-elliptic beating Hohmann at a radius ratio of 15; the plane-change geometry; and rejection of invalid input. New error `InvalidRadius`.
- `ephemeris`: the `Ephemeris` trait and `LowPrecision`, which gives the geocentric Sun in km in the J2000 ecliptic from the standard low-precision series (mean longitude and anomaly, with the first two equation-of-centre terms). Tests in `tests/ephemeris.rs`: the J2000 values (longitude 280.37°, distance 0.98333 AU), the perihelion and aphelion bounds over a year, one turn per tropical year, the daily motion, the ecliptic plane, and the output units. The Moon and planets are not written at this entry; see below.
- `ephemeris`: the Moon, from the leading terms of the low-precision lunar series (six longitude, four latitude, four distance terms).
- `force::SolarRadiationPressure`: solar radiation pressure from C_R and A/m, with the Sun from any `Ephemeris` and a cylindrical Earth shadow. Constant `SOLAR_PRESSURE_1AU_PA`. Tests in `tests/srp.rs`: the push is away from the Sun; the magnitude follows the law; linearity in C_R and A/m; the shadow boundary is exact at one Earth radius; invalid coefficients are rejected; a day of pressure moves a LEO orbit by a small, non-zero amount. New error `Negative`.
- `force::Thrust`: a constant thrust acceleration (km/s²), fixed in the inertial frame or along the velocity, active from `start` to `end` seconds past the epoch. No mass flow. Tests in `tests/thrust.rs`: direction and window (half-open), prograde alignment with the velocity, the degenerate case of a zero velocity, the impulse of a 10 s burn against a coast (3×10⁻⁵ relative at a 0.1 s step), and rejection of invalid input. New error `InvalidWindow`. The impulse test shows the sensitivity to the step at the window edges (3×10⁻⁵ at 0.1 s, 8×10⁻³ at 0.5 s), which the docs state.
- `visibility`: `GroundStation` (WGS 84 geodetic position and local vertical), `EarthRotation` (a start angle, advancing at the WGS 84 rate), `elevation`, and `contact_windows`, which samples a satellite's elevation at a fixed step and bisects each change to a millisecond. Tests in `tests/visibility.rs`: equator and polar radii against WGS 84, zenith and nadir, the window edges on the elevation limit, and a 500 km equatorial orbit over a day (15 passes of 237–474 s). New errors `OutOfRange`. Empty windows are dropped. The astrodynamics `dtn` feature placeholder is removed.
- `spk`: a reader for binary SPK kernels (NAIF DAF), for type 2 (Chebyshev position, velocity from the derivatives), J2000 frame, little-endian. `read_segments` parses the summaries from bytes the caller supplies; `SpkFile::state` gives position and velocity in km and km/s at an epoch in seconds past J2000 (TDB). Tests in `tests/spk.rs` build small kernels in the NAIF layout: summaries, record selection, Chebyshev values and derivatives against closed forms, the record boundary, not-found and out-of-coverage errors, unsupported types, and malformed, truncated and big-endian files. New errors `Malformed`, `Unsupported`, `NotFound`, `OutOfCoverage`.
- `sdp4` (deep-space terms, orbits of 225 minutes or more): the lunar-solar long-period terms (`dscom`, `dpper`), the one-day and half-day resonance coefficients (`dsinit`), and the resonance integrator (`dspace`), starting from the epoch on each call. Tests in `tests/sdp4.rs`: seven verification cases (28129, 26975, 08195, 28626, 09998, 04632, 20413) match the paper's printed states to 1e-5 km and 1e-7 km/s, except 08195, which has a 3e-4 km bound because of a residual that grows with time. An earlier draft of the tests took two rows from the wrong satellite block; they are replaced with rows whose dates match their epochs. `tests/sdp4_more.rs` adds the other deep-space and original Report #3 cases with printed outputs (25954, 24208, 21897, 22674, 09880, 23333, 28623, 16925, 26900, 23177, 23599, 14128, 11801, 88888) against a 3e-5 km bound: most agree to about 5e-9 km, and five reach 2e-6 to 1.7e-5 km. Element sets whose epoch token lacks the international designator (the original Report #3 sets) are read too.
- `sgp4`: the SGP4 propagator (near-earth, and deep-space through `sdp4`), with `GravityConstants::WGS72` (the default for the published tests) and `WGS84`, `Elements`, `parse_tle` for two-line element sets, and `Sgp4::new` and `propagate` (TEME, km and km/s). Tests in `tests/sgp4.rs`: the verification element set (satellite 00005) parses to its printed values; WGS-72 XKE matches the paper; the states at 0, 360, 720, 1080, 1440, 1800 and 2160 minutes match the paper's printed states to 1 mm and 10⁻⁸ km/s; deep-space element sets are `Unsupported`; malformed element sets are rejected. New errors `BadTle` and `Sgp4`.
- `sgp4` verification, `tests/sgp4_verification.rs`: the near-earth cases 06251 (normal drag), 28057 (low eccentricity), 28350 (perigee 127 km), 22312 (perigee 87 km) and 29238 (perigee 212 km, simplified drag) match the paper's printed states to 1 mm and 10⁻⁸ km/s at their printed epochs. The paper's trap claims hold: 28350 fails beyond 1500 minutes, 22312 at 2880 minutes, and the decaying case 29141 at 440 minutes; the sub-orbital case 28872 is refused at initialization.
- `atmosphere::density`: an exponential model of atmospheric density from 0 to 1000 km (piecewise, from a standard table of reference densities and scale heights; zero above 1000 km). Test: table rows reproduced, decrease with altitude over 1 km steps, the scale height between rows, zero above the table. The table's 25 km boundary is 0.14% off the row above it, within the rounding of the recalled values.
- `force::AtmosphericDrag`: drag −½·C_D·(A/m)·ρ·|v_rel|·v_rel with v_rel = v − ω×r (WGS 84 rotation rate), altitude above the WGS 84 radius. Constant `EARTH_ROTATION_RATE_RAD_S`. Tests in `tests/drag.rs`: zero drag for a body moving with the atmosphere; the force opposes the relative velocity with the expected magnitude; a circular orbit at 400 km loses between 0.25 and 0.5 km of semi-major axis in a day, against the textbook rate of about 0.36 km per day; and rejection of invalid input.
- `frames::ecliptic_to_equatorial` and `OBLIQUITY_J2000_ARCSEC` (84 381.406″). `force::ThirdBody`: the third-body perturbation μ_b((r_b − r)/|r_b − r|³ − r_b/|r_b|³), with the body's position from any `Ephemeris`, rotated into the equatorial frame, and time measured from an epoch. Constants `SUN_MU_KM3_S2` and `MOON_MU_KM3_S2`. Tests in `tests/third_body.rs`: the tide along the Sun line is 2μr/R³ and across it −μr/R³ (both within 1%); the perturbation vanishes at the Earth's centre; the ecliptic pole maps to the equatorial tilt; and a day of Sun perturbation moves a LEO orbit by a small but non-zero amount.
- `lambert::lambert`: Lambert's problem for zero-revolution transfers. Given two positions, a time of flight and a sense (`prograde` is counter-clockwise about +z), returns the velocities at both ends. Solved by bisection on the universal variable z, with the Stumpff functions from `propagate` (now `pub(crate)`). Tests in `tests/lambert.rs`: a 90° arc on a circular orbit and a 270° clockwise arc, both exact; round trips through the propagator for a prograde orbit and a retrograde one; and rejection of degenerate geometry (opposite, collinear, or plane containing z) and invalid input. New error `InvalidTimeOfFlight`.
